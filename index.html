About me

Where visual storytelling meets emerging technology.

I study Film and Media Arts and Artificial Intelligence, with minors in Animation Studies and Games. My goal is to create experiences that feel cinematic, intuitive, and genuinely human.

I’m especially interested in AI filmmaking, AI media, digital art, animation, and web development. I enjoy learning how a creative idea can move from an early concept to a polished experience on screen.

I have also collaborated with another student to develop an app and explore a startup idea. That experience showed me how much stronger a project can become through teamwork, feedback, and different perspectives.
